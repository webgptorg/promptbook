# TEAM

[Commitments](_index.md) · [Syntax](../syntax.md)

**Document:** Agent.

Describes, in natural language, whom the agent may consult and in which situations, with references such as `TEAM Ask @Lawyer about legal questions and @User about missing project intent.` It may reference project/hidden agents or Expert; User requests human input, and Void/Null is a valid no-op. Relationships may be reciprocal. See [consultations](../../cli/team.md).
