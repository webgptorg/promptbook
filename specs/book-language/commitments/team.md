# TEAM

[Commitments](_index.md) · [Syntax](../syntax.md)

**Document:** Agent.

Describes, in natural language, whom the agent may consult and in which situations, with references such as `TEAM Ask @Lawyer about legal questions and @User about missing project intent.` or `TEAM When you need to write book language consult with @Expert, to do the frontend tasks consult with {Frontend Developer}`. It may reference project/hidden agents or Expert; User begins/continues a durable chat needing human input, and Void/Null is a valid no-op. Relationships may be reciprocal. See [consultations](../../cli/team.md).
